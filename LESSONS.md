# Build lessons

The synthetic experiment validates that exact outcome aggregation can be reproducible and traceable, including when planted outcomes are changed. It does not validate the assumption that changing message structure improves outreach demand. Comparing two rates without equivalent targeting and observation windows is insufficient for that conclusion.

A domain lesson is that outreach outcomes form a funnel: a meeting implies a positive reply, which implies a reply. Rejecting contradictory rows is more useful than silently counting them.

Operating rule: keep fixture expectations independent of the engine, mutate planted inputs to prove computation, and require real-use evidence before expanding a tool.
